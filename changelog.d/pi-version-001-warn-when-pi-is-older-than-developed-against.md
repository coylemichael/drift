Drift warns once per session, as a Pi notification, when the running Pi is older than the version it is developed against (the floor of its `pi-coding-agent` dev dependency, 1.1.0 today): "Drift is developed against Pi 1.1.0; this is Pi 0.85.1. Update Pi where possible …". Nothing is blocked, and a current or newer Pi stays silent.
